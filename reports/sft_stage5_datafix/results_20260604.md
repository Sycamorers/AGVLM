## Decision

Do not promote Stage5 for the classification-fix objective.

Stage5 improves aggregate macro and clarify/respond relative to Stage4, but classification remains source-collapsed rather than image-discriminative. The new rice/banana data gave small nonzero gains, but IP102, PlantDoc, PlantVillage, Digital Green, and tea still collapse to one dominant prediction per source. The new OOS errors are concentrated in `digigreen_crop_disease` and `tea_sickness`, so their label spaces need inspection before another broad SFT round.

## Recommended Next Step

Before any further full SFT, run a controlled classification-only overfit probe:

- Start from the Stage2 adapter, not Stage4/Stage5.
- Use a tiny balanced train/eval slice per problematic source.
- Evaluate same-source greedy closed-label accuracy.
- Require near-perfect accuracy on the micro overfit slice before spending another full B200 run.

If the micro probe fails, focus on adapter application, prompt/label parsing, or optimization. If it passes, scale gradually by source instead of mixing all tasks immediately.
